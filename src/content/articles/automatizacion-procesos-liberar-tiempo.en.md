Every business has tasks that are necessary but repetitive and time-consuming: sending follow-up emails, generating reports, updating databases and so on. Process automation (RPA and beyond) means using technology so those tasks run on their own, freeing your team to focus on higher-value work.

## Key Areas to Start Automating

- **Marketing and Sales:** automate emails to new subscribers, lead follow-up, social media posting and customer segmentation. Tools like Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat) or CRMs like HubSpot are great for this.
- **Administration and Finance:** set up automatic recurring invoices, payment reminders and basic financial reports. Modern accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero) has many of these features built in.
- **Customer Service:** use chatbots to answer frequently asked questions 24/7, sort and assign support tickets automatically, and send satisfaction surveys after an interaction.

## The Automation Process in 3 Steps

1. **Identify and Document:** pick a repetitive, rule-based task with low cognitive value. Document every step of the current process.
2. **Choose the Right Tool:** you do not need to be a programmer. Many no-code or low-code tools let you build workflows by dragging and dropping elements.
3. **Implement and Measure:** build the automation flow, test it thoroughly and measure the result. How much time was saved? Were errors reduced?

Automation is not about replacing people but about amplifying what they can do. By removing the monotony, you let your team be more creative and strategic and focus on the real growth of the business.
